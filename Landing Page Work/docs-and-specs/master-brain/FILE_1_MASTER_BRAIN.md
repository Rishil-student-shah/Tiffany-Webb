She is moving **from** a job title someone gave her ("Gambling Prevention Coordinator") **to** a brand she owns ("Community Impact Strategist"). The website is the primary instrument of that shift. Every page should make her feel like a national figure, not an employee.

## 3.2 Her competitive white space
Nobody else occupies **culturally rooted gambling-harm prevention.** Her advantage is a rare three-part stack:
1. **Credentialed public-health expertise** (not just lived experience)
2. **Enormous frontline volume** — 4,000+ hours of actual outreach, not theory
3. **Cultural fluency** to reach communities standard prevention programs miss

Her one-line differentiator: *"The prevention leader who makes the hardest conversations approachable — and hands communities a plan."*

## 3.3 Why now (market context the site should imply, never lecture)
Gambling harm has become a recognized public-health issue as legal sports betting expanded across the US. There are very few credible, culturally fluent voices in this space. She is early, credentialed, and genuinely differentiated.

## 3.4 Brand architecture
**Tiffany Webb is the master brand.** Everything grows under her name:
- **Speaking** — the flagship, the site's primary conversion goal

- **GambleFreeGear** — an *endorsed sub-brand*: its own identity, visibly "by Tiffany Webb"

**Critical framing:** she is not the person behind GambleFreeGear. GambleFreeGear is one expression of her. The site must never let the apparel brand overshadow the speaker brand.

# 5. WHO VISITS THIS WEBSITE

Six real audiences, in priority order. The site is optimized for #1.

**1. The Conference Programmer** *(primary buyer — highest value)*
Books speakers for public-health, behavioral-health, and prevention conferences. Needs: proof she can hold a room, clear topics, easy booking. Decides months ahead, often by committee, and forwards materials internally. **The entire home page is engineered for this person.**

**2. The Coalition / Prevention Director**
Runs community coalitions or ROSC councils. Wants partnership, not a one-off talk. Modest budgets. Converts on evidence of community impact.

**3. The Healthcare / Behavioral-Health Decision-Maker**
Hospital community health, EAPs, treatment providers. Wants clinical relevance — screening, co-occurring disorders, referral pathways — and staff training.

**4. The School / University Administrator**
Student wellness, athletics, counselling. Wants youth-appropriate, parent-friendly programming on gambling and sports betting.

**5. The Journalist / Podcaster**
Needs bios, headshots, and facts without emailing her. The Media page exists entirely for this person.

**6. The Community Member**
Found her via social. Won't book her — becomes the owned audience that makes her bookable. Converts on newsletter signup.
