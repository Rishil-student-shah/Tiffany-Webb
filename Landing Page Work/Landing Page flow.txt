============================================================
GLOBAL ANIMATION PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Animation should make the website feel premium without
making the website difficult to use.

Use:

• Smooth reveal
• Image mask reveal
• Fade + translate
• Subtle scale
• Card hover
• Number counters
• Video reveal
• Soft section transitions


Avoid:

• Heavy 3D
• Full WebGL
• Constant parallax
• Excessive glassmorphism
• Large cursor effects
• Autoplay audio
• Excessive page transitions
• Animation on every element


RULE:

ONE MAJOR INTERACTION PER SECTION.

Animation supports the content.

Animation should never become the content.

FINAL WEBSITE PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

The website should NOT try to tell Tiffany's entire life story
on the homepage.

It should tell the visitor enough of the right story to create
confidence and move them toward action.

The homepage has one primary conversion:

                "INVITE TIFFANY TO SPEAK"

Books, GambleFreeGear, Insights and Media are supporting
parts of the larger Tiffany Webb ecosystem.

They should strengthen the personal brand without distracting
from the primary objective:

        DISCOVER → TRUST → INVITE → BOOK


FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE:

"Make the website feel like a premium speaker experience,
not a collection of website sections."

Every section should answer one question.

HERO:
Who is Tiffany?

ABOUT:
Who is she as a person and professional?

EXPERTISE:
What does she know?

SPEAKING:
What can she deliver?

SPEAKER REEL:
Can she actually do it?

IMPACT:
Who does she serve and what has she done?

PROOF:
Why should I trust her?

BOOK:
How do I hire her?

Everything else is secondary.
