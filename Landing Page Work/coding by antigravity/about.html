Meet Tiffany Webb

Community Impact Strategist · Public Health Educator & Speaker

Chicago soul, Louisiana heart.

Chicago-born and raised, with deep Louisiana family roots, Tiffany grew up between two places that shaped how she works: a city that taught her resilience, and a family culture that taught her hospitality — that you meet people where they are, and you feed them before you ask them anything.

Hers was a family that believed in literacy, healing, and empowerment. Books on the table, doors open, somebody always being helped. That is the inheritance she brought into public health.

She has spent more than fifteen years and over four thousand hours doing gambling-prevention outreach — in schools, clinics, community centres, and the rooms most prevention programs never reach. She came to it through behavioral health, and she stayed because she kept meeting families nobody had ever given language for what was happening to them.

Her work now blends behavioral-health expertise, bold community outreach, and an entrepreneurial streak that turned a personal mission into a brand people wear. Every piece of it points the same direction: turning pain into power for the people other programs overlook.

"When we rise, we rise together."
