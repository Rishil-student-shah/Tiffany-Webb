Approved Bio Copy

Short Bio (100 words)

Tiffany Webb is a Community Impact Strategist and Public Health Educator with over 15 years of experience. Specializing in gambling prevention and behavioral health, she transforms communities through evidence-based outreach. Known for her powerful message, "Break the Silence," Tiffany empowers youth, clinicians, and leaders nationwide.

Medium Bio (250 words)

With roots in Chicago and a heart for Louisiana, Tiffany Webb has dedicated her career to public health and grassroots community impact. For over 15 years, she has been a leading voice in gambling prevention, youth engagement, and behavioral health advocacy. Through her consultancy and keynote presentations, Tiffany addresses the often-overlooked intersections of mental wellness and community resilience. Her flagship initiative, GambleFreeGear, blends lifestyle branding with critical awareness campaigns. Tiffany has spent over 4,000 hours in direct community outreach, bridging the gap between clinical research and real-world application to foster systemic change.
