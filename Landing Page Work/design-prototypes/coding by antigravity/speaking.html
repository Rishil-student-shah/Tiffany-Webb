02
6 topics Treatment, Recovery & Clinical
+

A masterclass for behavioral health professionals on integrating gambling harm into standard intake and treatment protocols. Tiffany shares frontline clinical techniques.

Key Takeaways

  • Overcoming practitioner hesitation in addressing financial trauma.
  • Best practices for co-occurring disorders (substance abuse and gambling).
  • Building trust with clients experiencing high levels of shame.

Gambling is the hidden addiction. This session equips frontline healthcare workers with rapid, validated screening tools (like the Brief Biosocial Gambling Screen) to identify at-risk individuals during routine care.

An interactive workshop on applying Screening, Brief Intervention, and Referral to Treatment (SBIRT) combined with Motivational Interviewing specifically tailored for problem gamblers.

How to map and mobilize local resources to ensure that the transition from treatment to long-term recovery is supported by a robust community safety net.

03
5 topics Family, Special Pops & Impact
+

Gambling harm doesn't just affect the individual; it devastates the family system. This session focuses on financial protection, establishing boundaries, and healing family trauma.

Key Takeaways

  • Understanding the unique financial trauma associated with gambling.
  • Tools for spouses and parents to protect family assets.
  • Navigating the betrayal and rebuilding trust.

With the legalization of mobile sports betting, college campuses have become ground zero. Tiffany addresses the normalization of betting culture in sports and strategies for safeguarding student-athletes.

Examining the unique "escape" motivations for female gamblers, the accelerated telescoping effect of addiction in women, and how to create safe, destigmatized spaces for them to seek help.

04
5 topics Creative & Promotional Engagement
+

Drawing from the success of GambleFreeGear, Tiffany explains how physical artifacts and apparel can bypass stigma and start essential conversations in everyday environments.

Key Takeaways

  • The psychology of advocacy through personal style.
  • Creating campaigns that people want to engage with, rather than avoid.
  • How to launch an awareness sub-brand for your own cause.

When gambling operators spend billions on influencers, prevention must get smarter. How to leverage digital platforms to counter predatory marketing and reach vulnerable demographics where they spend their time.
