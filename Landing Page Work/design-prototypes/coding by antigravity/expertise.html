Areas of Expertise

Bridging frontline community outreach, clinical evidence, and health equity frameworks.

Evidence Base

Research & Data Anchors

NCPG National Data

National Scope & Suicidal Ideation

NCPG estimates 9 million American adults suffer from gambling problems with a national social cost of $14 billion annually. NCPG also notes 1 in 5 people with severe gambling problems attempt suicide (paired with Wardle et al. 2024 meta-analysis).

Peer-Reviewed Research

Domestic Violence & Financial Impact

Dowling et al. (2016 systematic review) found over 38% of problem gamblers report being victims of intimate-partner violence. UCLA Anderson & NBER 2024 working papers demonstrate increased bankruptcy and debt collection post sports-betting legalization.
