Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Tiffany Webb ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our website (tiffanywebb.com) and communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out forms on our website. This includes:

  • Booking Enquiries: Name, organization, email address, phone number, event details, and budget parameters submitted via our booking form.
  • Communications: Any information you provide when communicating with us via email, WhatsApp, or other channels.
  • Newsletter & Waitlists: Your email address if you subscribe to our newsletter or waitlists.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your booking enquiries and manage speaker engagements.
  • Send you updates, newsletters, and resources (you may opt out at any time).
  • Communicate with you regarding event coordination via email or WhatsApp.
  • Improve our website and services through aggregated analytics.

3. Third Parties & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business, including:

  • CRM & Email Providers: For managing contacts and sending newsletters.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Business API: We utilize WhatsApp Business API for operational notifications and communications. By providing your phone number, you consent to receive communications via WhatsApp subject to Meta's privacy policies.
  • Analytics: We use privacy-conscious analytics tools (such as GA4 or Plausible) to understand website traffic.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to analyze trends and track user movements around the site. You can control the use of cookies at the individual browser level.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: booking@tiffanywebb.com
