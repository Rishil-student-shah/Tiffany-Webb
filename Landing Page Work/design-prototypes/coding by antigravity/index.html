Meet Tiffany

Chicago soul, Louisiana heart.

Chicago-born and raised, with deep Louisiana family roots, Tiffany Webb has spent more than fifteen years doing prevention work where it actually happens — in schools, clinics, church basements, and community rooms across Illinois.

She came to this work through a family that believed in literacy, healing, and showing up for people. That belief still drives it. Whether she is training clinicians, speaking to a room of students, or sitting with a family in crisis, her approach is the same: start the conversation nobody else is starting, and give people somewhere to go with it.

Today she works with health systems, schools, coalitions, and government agencies to build prevention that reaches the people other programs miss.
