The three rules that keep this on schedule

Rule 01
Lock before you split
Nothing gets designed or built until Step 8 (brand lock) is done. Two people building from two interpretations is how decks and sites stop matching.
Rule 02
Ask early, ask once
Every ask from Tiffany goes out on Day 0 in one message. Her reply time is the only thing you don't control — start that clock immediately.
Rule 03
Two rounds, then it ships
Two revision rounds per deliverable, stated up front and kindly. Unlimited rounds is how a 30-day build becomes a 90-day one at the same price.
