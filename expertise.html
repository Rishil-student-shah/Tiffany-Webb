01

Gambling Prevention & Community Awareness

As legalized sports betting expands across the U.S., gambling harm is now recognized as a top-tier public-health challenge. Tiffany's prevention programming is rooted in cultural fluency — reaching communities that traditional outreach has historically overlooked.

Tiffany brings together evidence-based screening tools, motivational interviewing techniques, and authentic community storytelling to create prevention conversations that actually stick. Her approach is shaped by 4,000+ hours of direct outreach across Illinois communities.

Community Screenings Stigma Reduction Cultural Fluency Coalition Building

NCPG Estimate

Nearly 20 million U.S. adults — 8% — report indicators of a gambling problem (NCPG 2025 national survey estimate). This is a public health crisis demanding culturally responsive solutions.

02

Youth Gambling Prevention

The sports-betting boom has opened new vulnerabilities for young people. Tiffany's "Don't Bet on Your Future" programming delivers age-appropriate, culturally relevant prevention to students, youth groups, educators, and parents.

Her youth programs go beyond statistics — they create genuine conversations that help young people recognize risk, develop critical thinking around gambling promotion, and understand where to get help for themselves or someone they care about.

Book for Schools & Universities

Community Outreach & Coalition Building

From engaging elected officials to building prevention coalitions, Tiffany's outreach programming creates lasting community partnerships. Her ROSC Council engagement work helps coalitions develop sustainable prevention strategies that outlast any single event.

Public Health Education
