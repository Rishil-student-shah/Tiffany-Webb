## TYPOGRAPHY
- **Display / headlines:** an editorial high-contrast serif (Fraunces, or similar — Playfair-style but warmer). Large, confident, generous.
- **Body:** a clean modern sans-serif (Inter or similar). High clarity.
- **Utility / labels / data / numbers:** a mono face (Space Mono or similar) for eyebrows, stats, captions.
- Generous white space. Bold pull-quotes used as design elements. Serif = Sage authority; warm human photography = Caregiver heart.
